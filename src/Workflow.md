# Workflow

## Step 1

- READ THE CHALLENGE.
- Understand the data that you are trying to get.

## Step 2

- Download the image, look at it.
- Do you recognise it? Is it somewhere you know?
- Do you know anything about what is in the image?

## Step 3

- Run it through ExifTool, are there any notes on the image? does it have coordinates? is any info useful?

- Reverse image search it

## Step 4

- Google Dork things in the image or name or notes, hopefully by now you have a vague idea of where it is.

## Step 5

- Use all the information involved to continue, build a report, take notes.
